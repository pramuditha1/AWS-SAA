SAA-C03 exam overview

Official guideRead once

This exam checks that you can design AWS solutions that are secure, resilient, fast enough, and not wasteful — then improve a design that already exists. You do not need to memorize every API. You need to understand why a service exists and which constraint it satisfies.

Start here — the W questions

Why

AWS wants architects who pick the right building blocks for a business story: who can access what, what happens if an AZ dies, how to scale, and how to avoid paying for idle capacity.

What

65 questions (50 scored), 130 minutes, pass at 720/1000. Multiple choice and multiple response. Compensatory scoring — overall score matters, not a pass in every domain.

Where

Study path on this site: Foundations → high-weight Learn chapters (IAM, S3, VPC, EC2, ELB, databases, messaging) → Compare pickers → practice decoding question verbs.

Which habit scores

Meet every constraint in the stem first. Only then choose the cheapest or simplest remaining answer. Multi-AZ is high availability; Multi-Region is disaster recovery.

flowchart TD req[BusinessRequirement] req --> security[WhoCanAccessAndHowEncrypted] req --> coupling[StatefulOrDecoupled] req --> scale[ScaleVerticallyOrHorizontally] req --> ha[SingleAZ_MultiAZ_Or_MultiRegion] req --> cost[OnDemand_RI_SP_Or_Spot] security --> pick[PickTheSmallestFit] coupling --> pick scale --> pick ha --> pick cost --> pick

Question language — decode the verb

If the question says
most cost-effective

Spot, Savings Plans, S3 lifecycle, serverless, right-size. Drop over-provisioned or unused premium features.

If the question says
least operational overhead / operationally efficient

Managed and serverless: Fargate, Lambda, Aurora Serverless, RDS, S3. Drop self-managed EC2.

If the question says
highly available / fault tolerant

Multi-AZ, ELB, Auto Scaling, Aurora 6 copies, S3 11 nines. Drop single-AZ and single instance.

If the question says
secure / least privilege

Roles not access keys, private subnets, VPC endpoints, KMS CMK, SG not 0.0.0.0/0.

High-score habit For every practice question, write why the other three options fail. Distractors are valid AWS services used in the wrong constraint.
