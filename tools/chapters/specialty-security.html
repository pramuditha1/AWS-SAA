Security extras — RAM, CloudHSM, Artifact, Audit Manager, Detective

Should / nameDomain 1 · when KMS and IAM are not enough

These services appear when the stem has a sharp constraint: share a subnet without copying it, single-tenant HSM keys, download AWS’s own SOC report, map your evidence to a framework, or investigate a GuardDuty finding. Learn them as one-line answers.

Start here — the W questions

Why

Multi-account companies share network pieces. Regulators ask for AWS’s certificates and for your continuous evidence. Incidents need investigation graphs, not more raw logs alone.

What

AWS RAM shares resources across accounts. CloudHSM is single-tenant HSM capacity. Artifact hosts AWS compliance reports. Audit Manager maps your evidence to frameworks. Detective helps investigate findings.

Where

RAM shares are within an Organization (or invited accounts). CloudHSM clusters sit in your VPC. Artifact and Audit Manager are account/org governance consoles. Detective analyzes data in a Region.

Which pieces matter

Shared VPC/TGW via RAM · CloudHSM vs KMS · Artifact vs Audit Manager · Detective after GuardDuty. Stop there for SAA.

Case study 1 — shared subnets

Stem

App teams in separate accounts must launch ENIs into the same private subnets owned by a network account.

A — wrong Peering with copied CIDRs in every account.
B — correct Share subnets with AWS RAM (shared VPC).
C — wrong Exchange long-term access keys.
D — wrong PrivateLink for every EC2 instance.

Case study 2 — Artifact vs Audit Manager

Stem

Assessor wants AWS’s SOC 2 PDF, and separately wants your CloudTrail/Config evidence mapped to PCI all year.

A — wrong Macie for both.
B — correct Artifact for AWS’s reports; Audit Manager for your ongoing mapping.
C — wrong Only Detective.
D — wrong CloudHSM.

Tricky points

KMS vs CloudHSM. KMS is multi-tenant managed keys (usual answer). CloudHSM when the stem demands single-tenant HSM control; KMS can use CloudHSM as a custom key store when combined.

Artifact ≠ your compliance. Artifact is AWS’s paperwork about AWS.
